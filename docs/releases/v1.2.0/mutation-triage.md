# Mutation triage: v1.2.0 candidate

This is an adjudication of recorded experiments, not a claim that the original program contains every injected defect. A surviving mutation proves that its executed tests did not distinguish that changed program; a timeout proves neither survival nor semantic correctness. No production source was edited for this review.

## Frozen scope and accounting

The [adjudicated sample](evidence/mutation-adjudicated-summary.json) records **135 completed mutants: 80 caught, 43 missed, 7 unviable and 5 timeouts**, selected by round-robin shard `1/40` across eight crates. Initial adapter/proxy baselines failed before any mutant ran; their completed retry snapshots supersede those two initial rows only. The [original source manifest](evidence/mutation-source-manifest.json) and [retry source manifest](evidence/mutation-retry-source-manifest.json) identify the frozen sources. The original enumerated inventory was **5,226 possible mutations; the full space was not executed**. The latest per-crate inventories in the adjudicated mixed-snapshot rows sum to 5,241; this is inventory drift between snapshots, not additional executed coverage. These samples do not establish a population mutation score.

The later [directed critical run](evidence/mutation-critical-summary.json), with its [separate source manifest](evidence/mutation-critical-source-manifest.json), records **59 experiments: 30 caught and 29 missed**, with no recorded timeout/unviable outcome. Its per-crate selected counts are core 30, HDC 13, bus 10 and codec 6. Twelve full recorded names also occur in the original sample; three directed misses also occur among the original misses. Line movement creates additional semantic overlap (for example frame-fit line 456 → 462). **Do not add 135+59, caught totals, or inventories to report unique tested mutations.** No deduplicated cross-snapshot coverage total is asserted here.

Stable recorded names below include path, frozen location, function and mutation. Guard snippets/follow-ups identify semantic intent when lines move. Source links point to the current function location; manifests/outcomes, rather than current line numbers, establish the experiment's provenance. Subsequent source/tests may differ. A later ordinary test addition is never retroactively counted as `CaughtMutant`.

Classification: **G** = meaningful test/oracle gap for an observable contract or safety boundary; **P** = source-proven returned-value equivalence with a possible cost difference; **N** = no defect/equivalence established under the documented heuristic/fixture contract; **T** = recorded timeout requiring bounded direct follow-up. G is not an allegation of a defect in unmutated production source. Two P classifications below are narrowly scoped; no blanket equivalence waiver is given to other survivors.
